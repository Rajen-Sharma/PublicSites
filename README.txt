RNVV VENTURES — WEBSITE HOSTING BUNDLE

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Upload index.html and the images folder to your website public root (usually public_html or www). Keep their folder structure.
3. Visit your domain to view the website. Enable HTTPS through your hosting provider.

No build process, database or installation is required. All six photographs are included locally.
Only General Enquiries is shown: info@rnvvventures.com.au.
The email link opens the visitor's email app; the bundle does not provide email hosting.
